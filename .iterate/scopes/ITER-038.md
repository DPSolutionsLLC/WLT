# ITER-038: Music coordinator handoff and conductor approval

**Type:** Feature
**Status:** In Progress
**Plan:** plans/sacrament-music-handoff-and-approval.md
**Created:** 2026-09-30
**Group:** GROUP-02 — Sacrament planning flow (4 of 4)

## Summary
Finalizing a Sunday's topics tells the music coordinator — To Do, notification, and email if she
prefers — and the music she picks is submitted back to that Sunday's conductor to approve, or to
reject with a note so she picks again.

## Context
The music coordinator is in the app far less than the bishopric, so an in-app signal alone will
often be missed. Today she only sees a Sunday stop being dimmed ("Topics pending") on her Music
page; nothing tells her, and nothing comes back to the conductor.

## Desired Outcome
- When topics are finalized for a Sunday, the music coordinator gets **a To Do item and a
  notification**, plus **an email** if she has chosen to receive one, saying the topics are ready.
- She picks the music and **submits** it.
- The **conductor for that Sunday** reviews it: **approve** (done) or **reject with a note**, which
  returns it to her to choose again.
- Done when: finalizing topics produces her to-do, notification and (opted-in) email; her submit
  produces a review for the conductor; a rejection's note reaches her and reopens her work.

## Scope Notes
- **Email is a new capability for notifications** — today Resend sends only agenda and programme
  PDFs. An email preference is per person.
- Recipient is the conductor (see the paused f3b for take-over).
- Today's dimmed "Topics pending" Music card stays meaningful.

## Open Questions
- If a topic or pairing changes after she has started, is she told?
- Does the programme's own approval interact with the music approval?
