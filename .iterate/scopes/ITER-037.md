# ITER-037: Leftover candidates fill the rest of the rotation

**Type:** Feature
**Status:** Backlogged
**Created:** 2026-09-30
**Group:** GROUP-02 — Sacrament planning flow (3 of 4)

## Summary
When a conductor finalizes a Sunday and has candidates left over, the app offers their other open
speaking slots for the rest of their rotation to drop them into; anybody not placed goes back to
the pool.

## Context
A conductor weighing five or six people for one Sunday will often want the other two on a later
Sunday of the same month — rotations here are month by month. Without this, those names are lost
or re-entered by hand.

## Desired Outcome
- On finalizing speakers (ITER-036), unchosen candidates are offered the conductor's **other open
  slots for the remainder of their rotation** (normally their month; another bishopric member's
  rotation shape may differ).
- The conductor can place each leftover into one of those slots, or **drop** it.
- A dropped candidate **returns to the pool**, available to that conductor or anyone else later.
- Done when: finalizing a Sunday with leftovers offers the month's open slots, placing one fills
  that slot as a candidate (on its own Prayer Items, ITER-035), and dropping one leaves it in the
  pool.

## Scope Notes
- "Rotation" follows the conducting rotation, which can be weekly or monthly cadence.
- Whether prayers' leftovers get the same offer was not discussed — likely yes, ask.

## Open Questions
- What exactly is "the pool" — the same thing as the speaker suggestions list (who to ask next),
  or a new list of "considered but not used"?
- Is the pool shared across the bishopric?
