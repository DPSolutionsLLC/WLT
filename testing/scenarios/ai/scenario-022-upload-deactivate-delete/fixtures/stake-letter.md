# Letter to Ward Councils

**From the stake presidency — 12 February 2026**

Dear brothers and sisters,

We write to you at the beginning of a year in which we hope to simplify
rather than to add.

## On ministering

Ministering interviews are to be held **quarterly**, not monthly. Where a
ward has been holding them monthly and finds them fruitful, continue. Where
they have become a report on visits not yet made, change them.

The purpose of the interview is not accountability. It is to give a
companionship somewhere to bring what they have noticed.

## On sacrament meeting

We ask each ward to invite **youth speakers at least twice each year**, and
to give them the same notice and the same topic support an adult would
receive. A youth asked on Thursday for a talk on Sunday has been asked to
do something we would not ask of ourselves.

Wards should also review how far in advance speakers are invited. Two weeks
is the minimum that allows a speaker to prepare rather than to perform.

## On records

Ward clerks should complete the quarterly report within ten days of the
close of the quarter. Where a ward is carrying a vacancy in the clerkship,
please tell us rather than absorbing it quietly.

## On the coming year

We have one request above the others: that ward councils spend less of
their time reporting to one another and more of it deciding something. A
council meeting that ends without a decision has usually been a meeting
about a meeting.

With our confidence and our love,

*The stake presidency*
