A Sample Passage on Faith
Synthetic text for the Ward Leadership Tools testing harness.

This file is ORIGINAL TEXT written for the harness. It is not scripture and
nothing copyrighted enters this repository (CLAUDE.md §9). What the scenario
needs from it is only that it is tagged as standard works and that a search
for "faith" finds it, so that the standard-works exemption is something a
tester can see on screen rather than infer.

1 And now I would speak concerning faith, that it may be understood what
manner of thing it is, and how it differs from the certainty men often
mistake it for.

2 For faith is not a perfect knowledge of things. If a man has a perfect
knowledge of a thing, he has no need of faith concerning it, for he knows it
already, and knowledge has taken the place where faith once stood.

3 Therefore, if ye have faith ye hope for things which are not seen, but
which are true; and hope of this kind is not a wish, but an expectation
grounded in what ye have already been given.

4 And ye cannot know of a surety at the beginning. This is not a failure of
faith but the shape of it, for a seed is not judged in the hour it is planted
but in the season it bears.

5 Now, faith is a principle of action before it is ever a principle of
comfort. It asks first what a man will do, and only afterward what he will
feel; and they who wait for the feeling before the doing wait a long while.

6 And charity suffereth long, and is kind, and thinketh no evil; and faith
without that charity is a lamp with no oil in it, giving the appearance of
light and none of its warmth.

7 Wherefore, hold fast to what ye do know while ye wait upon what ye do not,
for the waiting is not idleness, and the not-knowing is not abandonment.
